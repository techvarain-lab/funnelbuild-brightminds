What happens next

Four steps from now

Today

We read anything you left in the booking notes and match your child to a tutor for their grade and subject.

Before the Saturday

Your tutor’s name and everything you need to bring arrive by text and email. If you booked online, we arrange a test call first.

On the Saturday

Arrive ten minutes early the first time. Stay as long as you like for the trial class afterwards. Plenty of parents sit in at the back.

Two or three days later

You read the written report. What is strong, what is not, what we would do first, what it costs, and what you can try at home tonight for nothing.

While you wait

Four things worth doing before Saturday

These cost nothing and take fifteen minutes. Worth doing even if the report tells you nothing you did not expect.

Ask them to explain it back One problem, out loud, without help. They will get stuck. That is the sticking point, and now you have heard it yourself.
Find the quiz papers The last month, including the poor marks. Bring them. They tell us more than the textbook does.
Pack for the visit Notebook, text or exercise book, pencil and eraser, and any teacher’s written comment.
Check the journey Dasmarinas, Cavite. Address and parking are in your confirmation, along with the join link if you chose online.

One Saturday, then you will know

No obligation at the end of it. If the report is not useful, you keep it and that is the whole story.
